The Roadmap

How we work.

No mystery, no long contracts before you know what you're getting. Four steps, in order, every time.

Step One

Assess

A free look at your digital house — what's built, what's missing, what's costing you. You leave with a clear picture whether or not we work together.

Step Two

Define

We settle the brand before we build anything. Who you serve, what makes you different, and how you want to sound saying it.

Step Three

Build

Design first, for your approval. Then the build. You see it in stages and you tell us what to change — nothing ships you haven't signed off on.

Step Four

Run

Launch is the start, not the finish. We stay on for the operations, the updates, and the follow-up that keeps the whole thing working.

Who You're Hiring

Hi, I'm Susy.

I spent twenty-five years inside corporate technology — business analyst, project manager, scrum master, agile coach, and eventually Director of Strategy and Delivery. My job was taking a plan nobody could execute and turning it into something that actually shipped.

Before that, I was in broadcast. Master control for television and radio — Univision, CBS 11, Radio Intelligence. I know what a story sounds like when it's cut right, and I know what it costs a business when nobody hears it.

In June 2025 I left the corporate world to do this full time. The first interviews were recorded from the front seat of my Kia Sportage, because that's what I had. Built in a car. Born in obedience.

Now I bring all of it — the strategy, the delivery, the broadcast, the marketing — to owners who were handed a template and told that was enough. It isn't.
