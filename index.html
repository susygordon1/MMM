Meet The Owner

Hey, I'm Susy Gordon. I came up in broadcast — master control for television and radio at Univision, CBS 11, and Radio Intelligence. I sat in the room where the signal either went out clean or didn't go out at all.

Then twenty-five years in corporate technology — business analyst, project manager, scrum master, agile coach, and eventually Director of Strategy and Delivery. My job was taking a plan nobody could execute and turning it into something that actually shipped.

In June 2025 I left to do this full time. The first interviews were recorded from the front seat of my Kia Sportage, because that's what I had. Built in a car. Born in obedience.

That's what you're hiring. Not a template shop — broadcast instincts, delivery discipline, and marketing that was learned on real airwaves before it was ever sold as a service.
